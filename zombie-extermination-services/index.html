Features

TOOLKIT

Mop, bag, grabber, fire

Four tools on the wheel and a blunt instrument for emergencies. Haul corpses by the ankles, burn them, scrub what soaked in, bag the rest.

CONTRACTS

Hand-authored sites

Every mess is placed by hand, not sprinkled by a generator. A diner, an office, a highway ambush, a residence, a rooftop. Each one reads like something happened there.

PRESSURE

Some of them get up

Not every body on the floor is finished. You will hear it before you see it, and you will be holding a mop.

SCORING

Three stars, one clock

Each contract has a par time. Beat it for a star, leave the place spotless for another. One star pays half. Three pays base plus the client's tip.

THE ROUND

Read the board, drive out

Pull a posting off the contract board, take the van, work the site, sign out. Nothing carries over except the money and what you learned.

REPEATABLE

Sites reset dirty

Take a contract again and the site comes back exactly as it was found. Same mess, better route, faster time.
