# AI Nudge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Data controller: GLukaz. Contact: lugiro43@gmail.com

## What data the app processes

**Data that stays on your device.** Your reminder settings (mode, times, days, quiet hours, custom messages) and usage statistics (streak and number of opens) are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to any server of ours: we don't have servers.

**Advertising (free version).** The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data in order to show and measure ads and, if you give your consent, to personalize them. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in the app. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Purchases.** The "Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play only tells us whether the purchase is valid.

## Who data is shared with

Only with Google (AdMob and Google Play), under the terms described above. We do not sell personal data.

## Your rights

You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app or by clearing its data from Android Settings. To exercise your rights with Google, see https://policies.google.com/privacy. For any questions about this policy, contact lugiro43@gmail.com.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
